# ADR-002 · 2026-04-29 · Dual MIT OR Apache-2.0 licensing

**Decision.** Hivecore is dual-licensed under MIT OR Apache-2.0. Users pick whichever applies to their downstream use.

**Context.** Open-source distribution requires a license. The Rust ecosystem strongly favors dual MIT-OR-Apache-2.0 because (a) MIT maximizes adoption with minimal compliance, (b) Apache-2.0 adds an explicit patent grant. Many downstream consumers (corporations) need the patent grant; many integrators (small teams) prefer MIT simplicity.

**Alternatives considered.**
- MIT-only — no patent grant, weaker for enterprise adoption.
- Apache-2.0-only — slightly heavier compliance, less common in OSS small-package world.
- BSD-3-Clause — fine, but less standard for the Rust ecosystem.
- Proprietary / source-available — incompatible with the OSS-first vision (see [vision.md](./vision.md)).

**Consequences.** Maximum compatibility with both adoption-focused integrators and patent-conscious enterprises. Contribution model: Apache-2.0 patent terms apply; no separate CLA needed.

**Revisit when.** A specific business model requires single-license clarity (e.g., commercial fork strategy with copyleft alternative).
